Awakened Proletariat

The Awakened Proletariat (AP) is a newspaper that I started on HomeworkSMP and is also the very first newspaper on the server.

The newspaper is filled with populist and anti-establishmentarian rhetoric and is extremely and obviously biased, to the point of hilarity (or at least I think it's hilarious), against the server owner (i.e. the establishment/bourgeois regime/whatever other term I come up with).

Issues are published on an ad hoc basis whenever something newsworthy happens on the server and will be posted here.
